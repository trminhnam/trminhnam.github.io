I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Purdue University](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/), advised by [Prof. Abulhair Saparov](https://asaparov.org/). My research focuses on reasoning in language models, including graph reasoning.

Before Purdue, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from the [University of Science, VNU-HCM](https://en.hcmus.edu.vn/), where I worked at the [Computational Linguistics Center](https://www.clc.hcmus.edu.vn/) on Vietnamese language understanding with [Prof. Dien Dinh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5tqOcFMAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Dr. Long HB Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jECXavQAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests include:

- **Reasoning:** how language models perform logical, multi-step, and graph reasoning, and how to improve it.
- **Reliable language models:** hallucination, robustness, and verifying model outputs.
- **Vision-language models:** multimodal reasoning and understanding.
- **Applications:** using language models for real-world and domain-specific tasks, such as question answering and information retrieval.

Feel free to [email me](mailto:minhnam.tran820@gmail.com) with questions or collaboration ideas.
